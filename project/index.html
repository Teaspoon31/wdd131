Fresh Food From Local Farms

FreshCart Local has been serving the Springfield community since 2014. We partner with family-owned farms within fifty miles to bring you produce, bakery items, and meat that are picked, baked, and packed within a day of arriving on our shelves. Browse this week's deals, build your order online, and pick it up curbside without ever leaving your car.

🥦 Produce

Seasonal fruits and vegetables harvested from local farms, restocked six days a week to guarantee peak freshness.

🥖 Bakery

Breads, rolls, and pastries baked fresh in-store every morning using traditional recipes and simple ingredients.

🥩 Meat

Hormone-free beef, pork, and poultry sourced from regional ranchers and cut fresh by our in-house butchers.
