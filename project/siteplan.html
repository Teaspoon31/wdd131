K3 Grocery Online

Site Name

K3 Grocery Online

This name was chosen because it represents a simple, modern online grocery shopping system that allows customers to easily order food items from anywhere.

Optional domain: k3groceryonline.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide an online platform where customers can browse grocery items, place orders, and receive digital receipts and order summaries.

Scenarios

  • How can I quickly order groceries for home delivery?
  • Where can I find the prices of fresh produce like fruits and vegetables?

Color Scheme

  • #2b6777 (Primary Color) – Used for headings and navigation
  • #f28c28 (Accent Color) – Used for buttons and highlights
  • #f5f7fa – Background color for page

Typography

  • Arial – Used for body text
  • Georgia – Used for headings (optional style variation)
