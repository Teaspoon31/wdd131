Website Planning Document

W05 Project Plan for FreshCart Local

Site Name

Name: FreshCart Local

Reasoning: This name was chosen because it immediately tells the customer what to expect: fresh, high-quality groceries and an easy shopping experience. The word "Local" emphasizes our relationship with neighborhood farmers and growers, giving the brand a trustworthy, community-centered identity.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the FreshCart Local website is to bridge the gap between our physical storefront and digital shoppers. The platform will showcase our weekly sales flyer, highlight fresh inventory arrivals from local farms, provide store hours and location details, and feature an online order form for convenient curbside grocery pickup.

Scenarios

The content and layout of this website are designed to directly answer the real-world questions of our shoppers:

  • Scenario 1: "What organic produce items are on sale this week, and are they currently in stock for curbside pickup?"
  • Scenario 2: "Where is the store located, what are your weekend operating hours, and how do I submit a pickup order online?"

Color Schema

The site uses a clean, appetizing color palette that mimics fresh vegetation and bright produce. This planning document intentionally uses these colors to preview the final site style:

Primary: #2E7D32
Headers, Main Buttons, Branding
Accent: #E65100
Sale Badges, Links, Cart Alerts
Text: #263238
Product Descriptions, Text Blocks
Background: #F9FBE7
Soft, Warm Canvas Background

Typography

To establish an inviting and professional digital storefront, we utilize two highly legible fonts from Google Fonts:

  • Poppins (Sans-Serif): Used exclusively for all main titles and section headings (<h1>, <h2>). Its clean geometric shapes give the store a modern, friendly look.
  • Inter (Sans-Serif): Used for all body text, bullet points, navigation items, and pricing lists. It is designed specifically for screen readability, making product lists and checkout forms easy to read.
